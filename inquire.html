How Booking Works

02

Proposal & scope

03

04

Before You Book

What to have ready

01

A date or a window

An exact date if you have one, or a season and a few options if you don't.

02

Rough guest count

Even a range helps shape the venue, rentals, and decor scale.

03

A budget range

The single biggest factor in matching you to the right tier and vendor list.

04

Any inspiration

Colors, photos, or a feeling you're chasing — nothing needs to be finalized yet.
