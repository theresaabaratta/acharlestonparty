About

A planner who sweats the details so you don't

Every party has a hundred small decisions hiding inside it — the linen color, the arrival time for the florist, where the cake table goes when the wind picks up. That's the part we take off your plate.

A Charleston Party works across the peninsula, Mount Pleasant, West Ashley, James Island, and Daniel Island, planning and styling private parties, showers, and small-to-mid-size gatherings. We build the concept, coordinate the vendors, and show up on the day to style the space and manage the run-of-show — so the only job left for the host is to enjoy the party.

How It Works

From first idea to last guest

01

Consultation

A conversation about the occasion, the guest list, the space, and a realistic budget range.

02

Concept & mood board

Palette, theme, and decor direction, pulled together into one board before anything gets booked.

03

Vendor coordination

Florists, rentals, catering, and entertainment sourced, booked, and confirmed on your behalf.

04

Timeline & logistics

A run-of-show that maps out load-in, setup, and the party itself, so nothing collides.

05

Day-of setup & styling

Decor installed and styled, room set, and details checked before the first guest arrives.

06

Send-off

Breakdown handled at the end of the night — nothing left for the host to clean up.
