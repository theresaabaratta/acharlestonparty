Discover Charleston

A city that keeps giving people a reason to gather

Charleston has been drawing visitors back to the same cobblestone streets and harbor views for three centuries, and it hasn't slowed down. Long before it's a party backdrop, it's a genuinely great American city — which is exactly why it makes such a good one.

Why Everyone's Talking About It

A city that's hard to stop ranking first

Charleston shows up near the top of nearly every "best of" travel list published each year, and has for a long time.

A City Built for Celebration

Charleston doesn't wait for an occasion

Between the rooftop bars over the harbor, the string-lit piazzas downtown, and a festival calendar that barely takes a month off, this is a city that already knows how to throw a party. A few fixtures on the yearly calendar:

Charleston Wine + Food March

A citywide food and drink festival that turns downtown into a five-day tasting event.

Spoleto Festival USA May – June

Seventeen days of opera, dance, theater, and music staged across the historic district.

Southeastern Wildlife Expo February

Art, outdoor culture, and a party atmosphere that spills across Marion Square and King Street.

Holiday Parade of Boats & Tree Lightings November – December

The harbor and historic squares turn into a season-long backdrop for holiday gatherings.

Where To Look

Neighborhoods worth knowing

Every part of the city has its own personality — and its own reason people end up celebrating there.

01

French Quarter

Gas-lit streets, courtyard gardens, and some of the most requested engagement-photo corners in the city.

02

King Street

Three districts of restaurants, rooftop bars, and boutiques that carry the city's nightlife after dark.

03

The Battery & Rainbow Row

Pastel antebellum homes and harbor views that have anchored Charleston's postcard image for generations.

04

Shem Creek

A working shrimp dock turned waterfront restaurant row in Mount Pleasant, best at sunset.

05

Daniel Island

A newer, quieter district with parks and event lawns that suit larger private gatherings.

06

Upper Peninsula

Former warehouses turned breweries and event spaces, the city's fastest-growing party scene.
