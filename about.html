About

A planner who sweats the details so you don't

A Charleston Party plans and styles private celebrations across the Lowcountry — from the first idea to the last string light.

Every party has a hundred small decisions hiding inside it — the linen color, the arrival time for the florist, where the cake table goes when the wind picks up. That's the part we take off your plate.

A Charleston Party works across the peninsula, Mount Pleasant, West Ashley, James Island, and Daniel Island, planning and styling birthdays, showers, engagements, and every gathering in between. We build the concept, coordinate the vendors, and show up on the day to style the space and run the timeline — so the only job left for the host is to enjoy the party.
